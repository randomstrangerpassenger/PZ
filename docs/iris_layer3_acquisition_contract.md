# Iris Layer 3 acquisition result contract

2026-09-04 · DVF-L3-04 · additive off-live authority. Owner execution/adoption
and the plan's P4 correction policy are approved by the implementation prompt.
The authority becomes adopted only through its exact successful G1 subject and
current route. L3-01/02/03 sealed definitions, code and corpus remain unchanged.

The predecessor human contract says open states do not create acquisition facts.
This successor clarifies, for L3-04 only, that an unknown state is never evidence
for a fact, while an independently confirmed fact survives an open question.
Open results retain empty terminal fact refs and separate partial contributions.
This is not a retroactive change to the predecessor bytes or definition revision 1.

The exact question universe is the 2,105 case-sensitive target FullTypes crossed
with `(acquisition, item)`. The bounded investigation inventory has six families:
`loot`, `vehicle`, `foraging`, `fishing_trapping`, `dynamic`, `transformation`.
Each family binds source members, full source observations, creation callsites,
require closure, reviewed consumer interpretation and remaining dependencies.
The shared survey is reused by all exact item queries. This is an L3-04 execution
inventory, not a new semantic taxonomy or a game-wide completeness declaration.

Coverage outcomes are closed: `found`, `not_found`, `interpretation_unresolved`
require performed source-member observations and consumer tracing;
`access_failure` and `not_attempted` do not satisfy investigation. Missing sources,
unknown outcomes, missing pairs, shallow survey records and cross-item refs fail
admission. A literal miss never proves unobtainability. Unqualified tokens remain
leads; the producer never adds a guessed `Base.` prefix or selects a duplicate
declaration winner. Indirect/constructed identifiers and unavailable engine
implementation remain explicit limitations of the bounded static survey.

All required families performed plus at least one independently confirmed path
means `resolved`. An unrelated uncertain additional path does not block it.
All families performed with no accepted answer means `investigated_unresolved`.
Any unperformed required family means `not_investigated`, even with partial facts.
Complete L3-04 adoption requires `not_investigated=0`. Acquisition resolved never
sets item complete; the existing `investigation.resolve_item()` formula does.

Positive `acquisition` facts contain a route and fact-local conditions, with
content-based IDs from the L3-03 identity function. Locator/review metadata does
not affect semantic identity. Equal meanings merge provenance; distinct paths or
conditions remain distinct. Provenance binds raw observation, source hash,
interpretation rule, exact item and exact fact. Reviewed rules cover registered
foraging definitions, ten literal generator templates, conditional crop-seed
outputs, fishing/trapping creation and registered new-player inventory paths. Runtime
conditions, random selection, spawn callback effects and engine boundaries are
retained. Raw distribution weights are not probabilities and raw place/vehicle
tokens are not user-facing names. Recipe output clauses and loot/vehicle tokens
remain leads where necessary namespace/engine truth is not established.

Source-specific caller readings distinguish actual delivery, returned existing
objects, preview-only factories, stash registration and nonordinary debug/test
injection. Distribution traces retain room/container procList references and
vehicle selection/alias chains; script traces retain result/replacement clauses
and exact callback definition lookup. Unreviewed trace obligations keep every
affected pair unperformed. A parsed callsite or a literal hit/miss cannot itself
supply reviewed semantics. Full source bodies are shared by observations; call
traces reference their enclosing function spans rather than duplicating bodies.

Foraging facts retain effective item and category defaults, zone/month/perk/
recipe/trait/tool predicates, square eligibility and action/delivery constraints.
Generated mappings are expanded only from the inspected literal loops. Fishing
retains later common-lure additions as well as individual record properties.
New-player paths retain starter-kit, difficulty, nested missing-schoolbag guards
and initial bat/hammer condition; the differently cased OnGameStart registration
does not establish a migration path.

Reviewed recovery rules also preserve world-generator/drum recovery, incidental
plow/shovel outputs, compost collection, padlock keys/return, splint/clothing/
curtain material recovery, candle replacement and broken fishing equipment.
These are broad acquisition facts with eligibility, not a copied Layer 4 action
list. Existing foraging Worm facts do not replace the independent plow/shovel
paths. Commented client EngineParts generation is excluded; the active client
command and server addItemOfType request are traced with the unavailable engine
message receiver as a specific remaining boundary.

`acquisition_unobtainable` requires complete item-global acquisition enumeration,
all family/source closure, explicit exclusion of dynamic/engine omissions,
source-bound closure observations, the inherited negative fields and no positive
contradiction. This snapshot has no such producer; zero negatives is valid.

The predecessor material set includes nonempty hint/origin, all acquisition
slot metadata plus supporting source refs, nonempty absence reasons, product
acquisition role leaves including empty refs, and every actual locale expression
surface as a conservative whole-span candidate. Recognized place claims retain
their separate text spans and locale comparisons. This deliberately includes
non-acquisition expression material in the census rather than losing prose with
empty role refs. Every material occurrence has source identity, locator and one of
`rebound`, `lead-only`, `unverified`, `rejected`. The current producer uses only
`lead-only`/`unverified`; no predecessor expression supplies accepted truth.

New implementation files are `acquisition_sources.py`, `acquisition_results.py`
and `acquisition_consumption.py` in the existing layer3 tooling package. The
immutable `corpus.json` and `manifest.json` live under
`Iris/_docs/authority/dvf/layer3_acquisition_results/`. The manifest binds those
new modules, this contract, one focused test, L3-01/02/03 inputs and protected
predecessor surfaces. Output is allowed only in that empty authority directory
or repository-local `.tmp/acquisition/`; existing nonempty subjects cannot be
overwritten. Corpus bytes stay candidate; route and successful closeout own
adoption. There is no composer fallback, runtime writer or completion writer.

L3-05 calls `acquisition_consumption.load(root, manifest_binding, mode=...)`.
The result contains both structured corpora and readpoints, revision, facts,
conditions, provenance, coverage/limitations and existing-resolver applications.
It uses L3-03 `application_inputs`, including all newly required questions,
partial bindings, pending routes and gaps. Modes cannot mix. The actual loaders
validate both readpoints before adopted consumption. Expression belongs to
L3-05; runtime/product adoption belongs to L3-06.

G1 is the single required identity
`test_layer3_acquisition_results.Layer3AcquisitionResultsTest.test_acquisition_results_contract`.
The current registry is `Iris/validation/execution/required_validations.json`;
the retained source discovery policy is
`Iris/_docs/round3/round3_pytest_source_classification.json`. The historical
required registry is preserved. No new taxonomy, proof tree or one-off validator
authority is introduced. Candidate full contract verification is followed only
by the limited adopted readpoint/combined consumption portion of the same G1.
The registry is actually read by `Iris/validation/execution/run_required_contract_tests.py`
and `run_repository_tests.py`, also by the public-text acceptance context and Lua
bridge exporter. The source-policy JSON is read by the existing description-v2
`tests/conftest.py`; its absence from direct authority/route entries does not make
that active discovery policy historical. Only its new source row and bound source
denominator change; existing shared entries are preserved.

If adopted evidence is defective, withdraw only its L3-04 route to the previous
valid readpoint (or initial absence), preserve immutable history and validate a
new correction subject before readoption. This preapproved P4 policy applies
only to this plan. No definition revision is authorized implicitly; a necessary
gap stays separate from unaffected evidence. Runtime, product prose, package,
installation, whole-game semantics and release readiness are outside this work.
